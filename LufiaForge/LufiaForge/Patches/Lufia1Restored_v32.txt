Within this archive are two patches.

   * Lufia1Restored_v32.IPS
      The primary project patch. Contains all features as listed in the overview.

   * AlwaysRetryMode.IPS (by justin3009)
      This allows players to start a game in Retry Mode (4x EXP/gold) without needing to unlock it
      first.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The sequels to Lufia & the Fortress of Doom reveal the dated aspects of the first entry.
Inconsistent terms and names, like the revival spell Dead or the elfin girl Mirka, and the
requisite slow walking speed on the world map and in dungeons.

When my part on Frue Lufia was done, I turned toward the first title to fix its worst issues.
Mercifully, D had released his censorship removal mod first. All it took was restoring certain
texts to match English canon. It would be remiss to not mention rainponcho's contributions of
restoring item descriptions and finalizing the walking speed improvements, nor Ok Impala!
reducing the encounter rate. And if you use Always Retry Mode? Please be sure to thank Justin!

Fans around the world came together to make the definitive editions of each Lufia title.
I sincerely hope that all players enjoy them.

Overview
========
GENERAL:
- Lufia Decensored used as a base.
   * Bunny girls return to Forfeit Island
   * Priests can be found in churches
   * References to alcohol restored
   * Gades' infamous line has been revised

- Dialogue printing noise now plays with Message Speed "Fast" enabled as in the Japanese original.
   (Credit to rainponcho)

- Game file expanded to 1.5 MB, increased from 1.0 MB original. (Credit: rainponcho)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GAMEPLAY:
- Increased walking speed on the world map and in dungeons. Includes the Falcon.
   (Credit: JCE3000GT, rainponcho, Justin3009)

- Cut enemy encounter rate on the world map and in dungeons by 50%. Includes the Falcon.
   (Credit: Ok Impala!)

- Final boss' battle script behaves as it does in the Japanese original. (Credit: rainponcho)

- Selan and Lufia can equip the following:
   * Zircon Rod
   * Zircon Robe
   * Zircon Wrist
   * Might Shield
   * Zircon Beret
   * Might Helmet
     (Selan begins the prologue equipped with the Zircon Rod and Zircon Robe.)

- Enemy Rock Golem now drops armor Might Shield.

- Hero learns spell Bravery at LV 2.

- Artea begins the prologue equipped with the Mach Heels.

- Audio defaults to Stereo when starting a New Game.

- (OPTIONAL) Patch to have Try Again Mode immediately, with no need to unlock. (Credit: Justin3009)

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

GRAPHICS:
- Crosses restored to church interiors. (Credit: rainponcho)

- 8x8 menu and 8x16 dialogue fonts changed to modified versions of those from Lufia II.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

TEXT:
- Item descriptions restored from the Japanese release. (Credit: rainponcho)

- Item, equipment, spell, and enemy names made consistent with Frue Lufia Trilogizer and
   Lufia: The Legend Returns Complete.

- Various small dialogue edits for consistency with the aforementioned projects.

   * Sweet Water/Foul Water cannot expire in towns, and their expiration notices properly appear.

   * Destroy

   * Wail
   * Melanchoy (Sorrowful)

   * Vulcan
   * Flood
   * Elegion (Thunder)
   * Dread


- Selan and Lufia can now equip: Zircon Rod, Zircon Robe, Zircon Wrist, Zircon Beret.

   Robe.


- Artea begins the prologue equipped with Mach Heels, rather than Cat Heels. This is to emphasize
