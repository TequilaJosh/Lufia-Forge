Just the Shadows - Turns the sickly green shadow and the thick gray
highlights on character sprites into a much darker, black color.

Version 1.0 - Adds material from Just the Shadows, as well as slight
re-coloring of character sprites to better match Lufia II: Rise of the
Sinistrals, and slight menu and dialog box re-colorings

Note: Due to the coding of field sprites, there is a slight discrepancy
between the colors used in Fortress of Doom versus Rise of the Sinistrals,
making complete fidelity impossible without better tools.
